Show us how you teach.

You will see COMLEX and USMLE board questions you have not seen before. Teach them out loud the way you would in a real session. Nothing to install and no account to make.

  1. Use a laptop or desktop, and share your entire screen. Your screen is how we see you work. A phone cannot share one, and a single window or tab hides the rest.
  2. Give the page your camera, microphone and screen. Your browser will ask.
  3. Two questions for each exam or topic you tick, on a clock. Each question gives you 90 seconds to read it and work it, then 5 minutes to teach it. The clock moves on by itself, so one exam or topic takes about 13 minutes and two take about 26 minutes.
  4. One take. Once you press Start the clock does not stop. Reloading the page, closing it, or coming back later does not give you more time and does not change your questions.
  5. Teach, do not answer. You get the vignette cold, with no answer, the same as the student would. Teach through it the way you would with someone who got it wrong and does not know why.
  6. A student talks back. While you teach, a second-year student who got the question wrong is on the page, in text and out loud. The page transcribes what you say so they can hear you. They answer what you ask and stay quiet while you explain. Ask them what they picked and why, and lead them to it. We are listening for the questions you ask, not only the explanation you give.
  7. You can use Google or an AI if you get stuck, though we would rather you did not. Your whole screen is in the recording and the page notes each time you leave it.
  8. Show an image from Google Images if it helps you explain something. Images, drawings and notes are welcome and none of them is required.

You can type anywhere on the question, highlight it, cross out choices, lock in your answer, and draw on a whiteboard or over the question itself.
