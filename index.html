You will see COMLEX and USMLE board questions you have not seen before. Teach them out loud the way you would in a real session. Nothing to install and no account to make.

  1. Use a laptop or desktop. Sharing your screen is how we see you work, and phones cannot share a screen.
  2. Give the page your camera, microphone and screen. Your browser will ask.
  3. Two questions, on a clock. Each one gives you 90 seconds to read it and work it, then 4 minutes to teach it. The clock moves on by itself, so the whole thing takes about 11 minutes.
  4. One take. Once you press Start the clock does not stop. Reloading the page, closing it, or coming back later does not give you more time and does not change your questions.
  5. Teach, do not answer. You get the vignette cold, with no answer, the same as the student would. Teach through it the way you would with someone who got it wrong and does not know why.

You can type on the question, bold it, highlight it and draw on the screen, the same way you would use a shared document in a session.
